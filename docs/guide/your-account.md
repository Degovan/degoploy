# Your account

Click your name in the top right to open the **account menu**. It shows your name, email, role and how you sign in (*Password* or the SSO name).

## Change the theme

In the **Theme** row, pick:

- **Light**,
- **Dark** (the default),
- **System**, which follows your operating system.

The choice is remembered in the browser you use.

## Change your password

For accounts that sign in with a password. SSO accounts see *Password managed by …* and change their password in the SSO.

1. In the account menu, click **Change password**.
2. Enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter a **New password**. The meter below shows its strength; it needs at least 10 characters and must differ from the current one.
4. Enter it again under **Confirm new password** until *Passwords match* shows.
5. Click **Change password**.

The dialog tells you how many other sessions (other browsers or devices) are signed in with your account. All of them are signed out once the password changes; the session you are using stays signed in.

> [!TIP]
> Forgot your password? Ask an admin to [reset it](users-and-invites.md#reset-a-users-password).

## Sign out

Click **Log out** in the account menu.
